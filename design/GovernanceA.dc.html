Who can do what, and how the console earns trust

These screens answer what a third-party auditor will ask first: who watches the watchers, who sees which patients, how late data is handled, and whether a decision can be changed afterwards. In the first release roles and users are managed in Keycloak. A Users section in Admin (next release) will do invite, role, disable and password reset from the console.

Build requirements that are not screensFirst release
Safe free textReasons and decision notes are shown as plain text and never as HTML. Forms carry a CSRF token. Tests try script text and markup in every free-text field.
Backup of decisionsThe console database holds the only copy of reviewer decisions. Daily backup with a restore test. Retention follows the audit retention (AR-23).
Tablet widthThe boards are drawn for a desktop. If reviewers use tablets, tables become cards under 900 px. To confirm with the Ministry.
